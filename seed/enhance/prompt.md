# BASE (always sent)
Edit this photograph. It shows a real, one-off handmade object. The object must stay exactly as it is. Change only the photography around it.

Change nothing about the object. Keep its shape, outline, proportions, size and position in the frame exactly the same. Keep every colour, pigment, pour, cell, drip, brushstroke, glaze, crackle, chip, wood grain, knot, joint, tool mark, scratch, signature and imperfection exactly where it is. Do not add, remove, repaint, sharpen into new detail, smooth, straighten, recolour, restyle, complete or "improve" any part of the object. Do not invent texture. If unsure, leave it unchanged.

Change only these:
1. Replace everything that is not the object with a clean, seamless, warm off-white gallery wall or studio sweep in colour #F3EFE7, with very subtle natural paper-like tonal variation. Remove clutter, floors, tables, hands, tape, other objects, cords and any text.
2. Light it like a museum: soft, even, diffuse daylight from the upper left, neutral white balance (about 5500K). Remove colour casts, harsh glare and hot spots. Keep the object's true colours as they appear under neutral light.
3. Add one soft, believable contact shadow where the object meets the wall or surface. No floating, no reflections, no vignette.
4. Keep the camera viewpoint, crop and aspect ratio of the original. Do not zoom, rotate or reframe. Keep the object the same size in frame, centred with even breathing room of wall around it.
5. Remove loose everyday items that are resting on or against the object but are not part of it (drink cans, cups, remotes, phones, keys, papers), filling the revealed surface with the object's own continuing material exactly.
6. Match the original colour saturation and contrast of the object exactly; do not boost vividness.

Output: one photorealistic, high-resolution, sharp catalogue photograph. No text, logos, watermarks, borders, frames or props.

# ADDENDUM painting
This is a flat painting or poured artwork. Show it front-on as an object hanging on the wall, edges visible. Keep the exact cells, lacing and colour boundaries of the pour. Do not add a frame if there is none.

# ADDENDUM woodwork
This is handmade woodwork. Keep the exact wood species, grain pattern, colour, finish level (do not make it glossier) and every joint. Place it on or against the same warm off-white sweep, standing on its real base.

# ADDENDUM ceramic
This is handmade ceramic. Keep the exact glaze colours, pooling, drips, crackle, speckles and the throwing marks. Control specular highlights so the glaze reads clearly without blown white spots.
