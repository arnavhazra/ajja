THE MAKER'S CREDO // AJJA NARAYAN RAO (B. 1946)

On letting the wood decide the grain.

“When I was twenty, I tried to force the timber to submit to my drawing. Now that I am eighty, I know the tree has lived three hundred monsoons longer than I have. My only task is to clear away the silence so its grain can speak.”

Nothing in the Chamundi Foothills workshop has ever known mechanical sandpaper speed, synthetic polyurethane, or chemical stains. Timber surfaces are trued using hand-forged Japanese smoothing planes (kanna) and local high-carbon steel gouges, then burnished with dried horsetail reed stalks and unrefined cold-pressed sesame oil.

Each ceramic vessel is thrown on a kick-wheel from Kaveri river silt clay and fired for seven days in a wood-stoked kiln, where falling teak ash melts naturally on the shoulders of the pot to form an unrepeatable mineral glaze.

01. Zero Chemical Binders

Joined solely through hand-cut sliding dovetails, wedged mortise-and-tenon, and seasoned rosewood pins. Pigments are mulled by hand with raw linseed oil and dammar tree resin.

02. Perpetual Studio Custody

Every artifact carries a lifetime re-waxing and conservation guarantee at our Mysore bench at zero cost to the custodian or their heirs.

CUSTODIAL TRANSPARENCY & LOGISTICS

Deed of Transfer & Archival Crating

How each one-of-a-kind work is sourced, authenticated on hand-loomed cotton rag, and crated in custom pine for safe passage from Mysore to your home.

We never fell living trees. All timber in the archive—Nilgiri teak, Malabar rosewood, Eastern white cedar, and wild jackfruit—is salvaged from trees naturally uprooted during the southwest monsoon along the Kabini and Kaveri river basins. Logs are air-dried under shade in our Mysore courtyard for 8 to 14 years until equilibrium moisture content reaches a stable 8.2%.

• MOISTURE EQUILIBRIUM: 8.2% STABLE • CLAY SOURCE: KAVERI DELTA SILT • PIGMENTS: NATURAL EARTH & MINERAL OXIDES

Accompanying every acquisition is a physical archival folio containing a hand-typed Certificate of Provenance on 300gsm cold-pressed khadi cotton rag, embossed with the Mysore Studio red lac wax seal, Ajja’s wet-ink signature, the exact date of completion, and a 30ml glass apothecary vial of our studio beeswax-sesame maintenance balm.

• DEED PAPER: 100% KHADI COTTON RAG • INCLUDES: 30ML BEESWAX CARE VIAL • ARCHIVE REGISTRY: PERMANENT LEDGER ENTRY

Within 72 hours of acquisition, our studio carpenter constructs a custom ISPM-15 certified pine crate tailored to the exact geometry of your piece, suspended in acid-free glassine tissue and high-density natural coir cushioning. Dispatched worldwide via DHL Express Priority Air or BlueDart White-Glove with 100% marine & transit insurance included.
